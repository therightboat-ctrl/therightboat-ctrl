### How I approach a project

- **Start with the problem.** Understand who needs the information or tool, and what decision it should support.
- **Make complexity approachable.** Use clear visual hierarchy, purposeful interactions, and concise explanations.
- **Build for reliability.** Favor readable code, deliberate testing, and maintainable solutions.
- **Iterate with evidence.** Check the experience across layouts and refine what's confusing or fragile.
